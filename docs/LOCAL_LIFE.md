## Household budget example

The previously approved 1,368 CR monthly expense example can be itemized as 650 rent, 320 food, 120 utilities, 60 transport, 60 healthcare, 58 household supplies and 100 contingency. The contingency is a budget provision, not a payment until spent. At 160 paid hours × 12 CR/h, gross 1,920 CR leaves 552 CR before deductions. Actual household rent varies above; monthly and weekly payroll scenarios use different reference periods, not a fixed four-week calendar month. No additional welfare or wage entitlement is implied.

## Transaction and simulation contract

Each transfer has a unique ID, fictional time, source, recipient, amount and provenance; debit and credit reconcile once. Opening reserves are explicitly seeded, not repeated income. Work creates an earned obligation only under the agreement and actual compensable work; settlement draws from funded accounts or leaves a payable. Purchases consume actual stock/services and money; obligations and settlement remain separate. Do not double-count projected revenue or expense as actual cash. All NPCs retain independent memory, private balances and evidence-based knowledge. No player or NPC can teleport or double-spend time. No automatic work completion, guaranteed vacancy or forced NPC encounter.
