# Ability domain contract — v0.1 draft for peer review

Baseline f75bdd14827bd6ea8555f6ca428132e91cff97c3. Status: specification and inactive draft fixture only. No gameplay activation, owner approval or final balance values are implied. Three pilot concepts are proposed for mechanical review; none counts toward the accepted 10,000 until reviewed and implemented. The proposed 100-record pilot remains a later target.

## Design and taxonomy

Separate core mechanism, manifestation, technique, numbered technique level, seven-tier mastery, general experience, natural attributes, superhuman modifiers, learned field proficiency, and known information. A mechanism is a causal operation on a world target, not a name or stat bonus. Initial candidate families: material/interface, energy transfer, motion/geometry, information/sensing, biological support, environmental manipulation. Family labels alone never establish uniqueness. Use stable AB-00001–AB-10000 IDs, never recycle after approval. Draft fixture IDs are reservations subject to review, not grants.

Initial typed primitives (proposed): traction_bias(existing_contact, direction, bounded coefficient change), echo_measurement(returned_self_emitted_sound, arrival evidence, uncertainty), heat_relay(two_touched_nonliving_solids, signed heat transfer, rate cap). Each needs feasibility, units, bounds, observable consequences, interruption and counterplay. These are simulation abstractions; no production handlers exist. Heat transfer must conserve modeled heat, traction must not create free motion, echo measurement must not reveal hidden truth. For expansion require distinct causal affordances and counterplay, not mere different scalars, targets with synonyms, visual effects or technique levels.

## Draft catalog data

See data/abilities/pilot_00001_00003.json. Envelope: version, status (draft only in this fixture), abilities array. Each record: stable ID, name, category, mechanism triple (primitive, target, operation), activation, limits, counterplay, manifestation parameters, acquisition route eligibility, root, owned nodes and private conditions. Node IDs are scoped to their ability. Each node specifies type, numbered level, mastery tier 1–7, prerequisites, private conditions and effect description. Root is level 1 with no prerequisite or hidden condition. Conditions are typed event evidence, minimum distinct committed evidence occurrences, scope, once-only and hint; hint is not automatically visible. The sample thresholds are synthetic review values only. Nodes at higher levels must retain the preceding level as a prerequisite. This fixture does NOT yet specify numerical range, duration, phase, costs, rarity, caps or complete seven-tier depth; it must not be admitted into a runtime catalog. Future machine schema must require those fields, units, versioned primitive references, species/target eligibility and explicit visibility rules before activation.

## Acquisition and progression proposal

Actor private state: actor ID, revision, intended candidate ID (not acquired), nullable acquiredCoreId, acquisition event ID, manifestation parameters, learned node IDs, technique progress, mastery tier, discovered condition IDs, evidence receipts, catalog version. No core use before acquisition. The initial character's age-17 birthday fixes the universe phenomenon; age-18 natural awakening milestone resolves once per eligible actor in authoritative history, not once per creation screen. Natural awakening is chance-based, not guaranteed; exact probabilities, NPC candidate distribution and earlier/later exception rules are pending. A natural success grants the eligible intended candidate. A failed milestone records its outcome; technical recovery must not reroll it. An unawakened post-phenomenon actor may access a glowing meteorite, choose only from its type's approved candidates, and satisfy its acquisition conditions; possession is not success. One core maximum; no replacement or second grant. Meteorite availability/depletion, transaction contention, chance stream, actor eligibility and type mapping require peer review. No draft type ID is actual world content.

Progress comes from deduplicated committed relevant evidence, not preview, repeated UI or interrupted unearned work. Practice at age 40+ primarily maintains ordinary natural attributes; no automatic birthday decline, natural ceiling 20; enhancement separate. Field skills, techniques, mastery and general XP are independent. Rare passive: natural trained trait within natural cap or modifier of acquired core; not an independent power. No guaranteed reward for harm, no stamina/mana meter. Balance curves and rare unlock rates remain pending. World rewind restores historical ability state as part of the whole world; technical retry does not duplicate effects. Divine Judgement belongs outside this domain.

## Authority and integration (proposed, not existing APIs)

01 owns world time, RNG, revision checks, scheduler and atomic effect commits. 02 owns biology, injury, inventory, ownership, recovery and actor knowledge. 03 supplies versioned private definitions and pure proposals, never writes authoritative stores or advances time. 04 reads only authorized actor-scoped projections. Request envelope: world ID, actor ID, attempt ID, expected revision, catalog version, tick with world-owned time unit, technique/route ID, target ID and trusted context references. Result proposal: required reservations, phase plan, typed domain effects, evidence and observation candidates, or rejected reason. No partial commit on stale revision, invalid target, unknown/unapproved definition, duplicate attempt, failed prerequisite, insufficient range or impossible phase. Distinguish rejected admission from resolved miss/interruption with actual elapsed time. 01 must coordinate atomic revalidation and commit; 02 resolves biological and property consequences. Replayed committed event must not award new progression. Existing executor accepts abstract phases/ticks and an execution marker, not actual damage/geometry; no invented API binding. Define exact units with 01 before timing implementation (integer milliseconds are a candidate, not a chosen world unit).

## Combat and creatures

Use actual positions/reach, relative movement, observation, preparation, release, contact, recovery, effective attributes, learned proficiency and anatomy. Reaction is possible only if the threat is perceived and response latency plus feasible movement fits available time. Revalidate geometry and contact at boundary; a defense choice is not guaranteed success. A committed attack is not undone by recovery cancellation. Settle simultaneous contacts under a reviewed world tie policy, not player priority. Propose one causal damage receipt; 02 derives HP, local injury and functional impairment without double subtraction. Zero HP is not automatic death. Species-specific anatomy and autonomous creature needs, growth, reproduction and migration use actual world capacity and time. Rifts retain actual location, duration, entry/exit, ecology, clearance evidence and unknown conditions. No automatic clearance notification without completion. No player-level scaling. High-tier civilizations and rare permanent stabilization COMING SOON. Exact combat formula, units, phase ties, rift exit/closure and damage/recovery thresholds remain pending; not implemented.

## Disclosure

Public catalog may expose approved discoverable descriptions; actor capability projection contains only acquired known techniques and authorized observed facts, not undiscovered node IDs, condition predicates, thresholds, RNG draws or NPC secrets. Intended candidate can be shown as locked during creation; no future capability guarantees. Offline client files cannot guarantee secrecy against direct file inspection. Agent 04 must receive safe projection fixtures after review.

## Acceptance and review gate

Structural fixture checks: ID/name/mechanism triple uniqueness, valid references, graph reachability/acyclicity, tier and level constraints, condition scope and ownership. Semantic review separately tests genuine distinctness and limits/counters, approved acquisition, no second power, no stamina/mana, no hidden leaks, conservation, natural ceiling, phase timing, rollback and same-event deduplication. Before gameplay implementation: finalize primitive units and bounds, schema and complete per-record fields, sample acquisition/progression receipts, review by 02 and interface review by 01/04. Require owner documentation review under the charter. No activation until gates pass. Next bounded task: formalize machine-readable schema and validator with negative cases, after peer feedback; fill range/duration/phase and effect constraints rather than mass-producing names.
