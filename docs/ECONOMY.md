# GODSEED — Currency and financial knowledge

Owner-approved direction, 2026-11-26.

## Credits and provenance

- The currency is Credits. Track clean (legitimate) and dirty (illicit-origin) funds separately. Proposed display notation: CR for clean and DCR for dirty. These are accounting classifications of the same underlying unit, not two unrelated currencies.
- Clean and dirty funds are not determined by alignment. A hero can hold illicit-origin funds; a villain can earn legitimate income. Track origin and balance in authoritative financial state rather than inferring origin from a character's title or reputation.
- The split is visible to the player in their own financial status. Do not reveal private balances or provenance to NPCs merely because a transaction occurs.
- A normal payment does not automatically disclose illicit origin or fail solely because the funds are dirty. Acceptance, scrutiny and consequences depend on the actual transaction, applicable institutions, evidence and independently acting NPCs.

## Sensitive circumstances and perception

- The owner approved conditional discovery rather than automatic NPC knowledge. The owner's additional suggestion is that an NPC's superior perception or similar capability could help detect suspicious circumstances.
- Proposed resolution rule: perception helps notice available sensory clues; awareness helps identify contextual irregularities; reasoning and relevant financial or investigative expertise help interpret evidence. These are distinct roles, not multiple automatic bonuses for the same check.
- Sensitive circumstances may warrant scrutiny, such as a regulated transaction, an authorized financial review, or other evidence of illicit involvement. These are candidate fictional triggers, not finalized thresholds or a guide to real-world financial evasion.
- High perception alone does not expose hidden ledgers, prove a crime, or let an NPC know something that has no observable or otherwise accessible evidence. Suspicion is not proof. Record each NPC's acquired knowledge, uncertainty and evidence source separately; subsequent behavior depends on their own goals, duties and decisions.
- Do not globally broadcast suspicion to other NPCs. Information transfers require an actual supported communication or evidence-sharing event. Avoid forced cooperation or universal hostility based on dirty funds.
- Exact detection checks, trigger thresholds, transfer rules, legal consequences and payment resolution remain open. Preserve ordinary NPC autonomy and information boundaries.

## Interface

The header's center retains character name, current/max health and currency. Proposed compact display: 250 CR · 80 DCR. In the full status panel, label both balances explicitly. The values are examples, not starting funds. Final compact layout remains subject to mobile review. Physical and digital payment support was proposed but not finalized.

## Purchasing power and initial economic reference — approved 2026-11-26

One Credit has approximately the purchasing power of one modern US dollar as a familiar design reference, not a real-world exchange rate. This applies to the nominal unit regardless of clean or dirty provenance.

Clean and dirty Credits have the same nominal value; underground prices may differ due to scarcity, demand and risk, not an automatic currency multiplier. Preserve the separate provenance ledger and evidence-based NPC knowledge rules.

## Persistent assets and code-protected bank storage — owner-approved (2026-11-26)

After a character dies, possessions remain in the persistent world. Inheritance depends on relationships, laws and circumstances; assets can be stolen when circumstances permit. There is no automatic account-wide transfer of personal possessions, money, level, abilities, stats or mastery. The owner explicitly permits a character to create a bank account, store assets behind a code, and use the code later to unlock the stored assets, including as a successor-access mechanism. This does not grant ownership of every estate asset or transfer personal progression.

Proposed safeguards: use persistent account/storage identifiers, actual deposited asset records, and atomic access/withdrawal transactions; do not duplicate assets or reset their provenance. Preserve separate clean/dirty Credit balances and evidence-based NPC knowledge. Access should depend on the defined code-protected arrangement, not automatic universal knowledge or player ownership of the world. Theft is a possible simulated event, not guaranteed loss. Exact storage types, ownership/beneficiary rules, code setup and knowledge exception for successors, bank procedures, access failures, fees and security mechanics remain open. Do not assume a real-world banking procedure or add a real credential requirement. See [GAMEPLAY.md](GAMEPLAY.md). Documentation only; no runtime implementation or tests performed.
