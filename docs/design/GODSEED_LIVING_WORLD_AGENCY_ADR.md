# GODSEED ADR — Living world, autonomous agents, no chapter progression

Status: **Accepted product decision** (owner instruction, 2026-10-20).
Implementation: **incremental**; current slice on `feat/godseed-v3-living-agency-20261020` is not the finished civilization engine.
Supersedes **as a product direction** any design requiring chapter flags, scripted NPC compliance, fixed quest order, authored capitals, predetermined kings or obligatory story endings. Does **not** delete or silently break historic saves and old standalone prototype artifacts.

## Decision

GODSEED is an open-ended living-world / god-observer simulation. The player observes independent life and may later provide *nonbinding* divine suggestions; NPCs decide whether and how to act. **No chapter system, chapter completion condition, plot gate, scripted victory, authored route sequence, mandated kingdom or forced civilization era** belongs in the new living-world gameplay.

"Free will" in a deterministic software simulation means **bounded simulated agency**: individuals select among available feasible actions using needs, preferences, relationships, skills, past outcomes, knowledge, physical risks and opportunities. Software necessarily defines action physics, consequences and feasibility. The game must not contain an omniscient director choosing what each NPC should do or a hidden mandatory timeline.

The world's initial terrain and resources may be generated by a reproducible seed. **Player camera position must not create, command, despawn or advance NPCs.** Every completed construction requires a site, work, physical resources and preserved ownership. Survival, starvation, rest, death and failed efforts have real consequences. Different seeds, characters, circumstances and choices should produce different settlements.

## Engineering acceptance contract

1. **Agent decision:** evaluate feasible candidate goals on current world state; report chosen action, reason and alternatives. Selection must be individual (preferences/needs differ), deterministic under the same inputs, observable, and not coupled to an authored chapter/tick milestone.
2. **Agent agency:** no single prewired "must build house, then road, then kingdom" sequence. Available opportunities and necessities compete. NPCs can switch priorities, defer work, explore, recover, decline optional activity and respond to changed conditions.
3. **Place autonomy:** construction locations are selected from accessible terrain/adjacent resources, rather than magic constants, without teleporting supplies or producing structures when the player observes a region.
4. **Conservation:** wood spent on homes/roads is debited from gathered physical resources; shared resource edits remain persistent after chunk unload and reload. Save tampering must fail closed, and old saves must not be rewritten without a safe migration.
5. **History:** milestones (arrival, construction, death) are preserved while routine memories remain bounded. NPC intent, personal characteristics and life decisions must persist across save/load.
6. **Scalability:** ultimately support multiple inhabitants, births, migration, crafts, roads, farms, trade, diplomacy, settlement growth, kingdom formation, succession and potentially other kingdoms; choose offscreen budgets to run on mobile with the same logical outcomes, subject to verification.
7. **Kingdoms are emergent outcomes:** no hardcoded named kingdom, capital, ruler or number of kingdoms. A kingdom exists only when real settlements, institutions, cooperation and territorial relations satisfy transparent world rules. Future dimensions/layers belong to persistent places and institutions, not story chapters.
8. **No chapters anywhere in the living-world loop:** a simulation tick is not a chapter. A chronicle is a *record of what occurred*, never a checklist telling actors what must occur.

## Implemented in this experimental vertical slice

- `src/v3/agency.js`: reusable person-specific bounded preference profile; legal-action utility evaluation; explicit reason, alternatives, life memories, validation. **No network LLM required**, no authored quest nodes.
- `src/v3/infrastructure.js`: independently scores reachable home plots based on actual terrain, distance and agent preferences. Original autonomous road site planning continues to use actual structures.
- `src/v3/expedition.js`: frontier NPC Mira evaluates available jobs; survival still has necessary physiological consequences. Homes use a persisted personally chosen plot; roads consume one physical timber/tile and take actual travel/work. She can optionally explore or reflect after construction.
- `src/v3/world_sim.js`: schema v4 living-world records with migrations from schemas 1, 2 and 3; prior built structures remain exactly where they were. Legacy mode/save keys remain untouched.
- UI and `GODSEED-V3-Play.html`: opt-in `?mode=world` remains a chapterless offline world spectator; HUD exposes chosen job, curiosity/security and personal memories. `mode=solo` and `mode=society` still exist only for backward compatibility with the historical prototype.
- New and updated `tests/v3_agency.test.js`, `tests/v3_world_npc.test.js`: preferences affect feasible choices, save/load stability and tamper rejection, terrain-based sites, durable founding memories.

## Technical debt not to misrepresent as free simulation

- Taren's prior 224-waypoint itinerary, workshop/tick spawn trigger, seed-favored regional plazas, and existing prelaid ancient roads are **hardcoded legacy behaviors**. They are not evidence of independent exploration/road development.
- The homeland's preexisting fixed building sites, founder IDs, council triggers and second-community coordinates are **scripted prototypes**. They must be replaced or isolated behind compatibility, not copied into the general kingdom engine.
- Mira is only one frontier agent on this branch; the current road planner links a house with a camp; this is not multi-agent settlement construction, general government, trade, farming, population reproduction or kingdom diplomacy.
- A deterministic utility-based planner provides bounded *simulated* autonomy, not unrestricted freedom or an AI that learns arbitrary capabilities. New capabilities must be implemented, constrained and tested.
- The 1,024×1,024 V3 area is finite in the current source. Removing artificial local planning budgets does **not** create infinite terrain.

## Continuation priorities (implementation backlog, not story chapters)

**Now:** Finish dependable NPC choice + dynamic site selection + persistence, remove per-NPC hardcoded decisions one subsystem at a time.

**Next:** NPC-agnostic actor lifecycle and available-action registry; independent inventories, knowledge, work projects, real building sites, offscreen decisions and collision-safe use of multiple workers. Replace Taren's itinerary and scripted arrivals with interest-driven opportunities, relocation and local communication.

**Later:** Unscripted settlements with food/water/road economies, multi-NPC construction, trade, population changes, voluntary councils, laws/legitimacy and real political borders. Only then implement self-formed kingdoms, succession, conflicting allegiances and multiple kingdoms/dimensions. Avoid mandatory chronology, predetermined capitals and authored "kingdom complete" cutscenes.

## Isolation, evidence and governance

Branch is stacked on `feat/godseed-v3-emergent-roads-20261020` (draft PR #65); **no merge to main**, no ownership claim in `docs/agents/tasks.json`, and no overwriting other bots' claims. Before integration, reconcile existing CIV/SIM/WLD task owners and V3 branches with the coordinator. QA evidence must identify exact head SHA and commands; passing Node tests does not certify Android physical performance. Never declare the whole game chapter-free while the legacy story prototype remains distributed: the decision governs the **new living-world experience**, and older story artifacts should ultimately become archived/optional without destroying saves.
